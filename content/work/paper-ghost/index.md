The work covers 15 major film + TV productions, with interactive props, screen visuals, projection playback, and set previsualization tools.

Production involved direct collaboration with directors, producers, and VFX supervisors. [Zed Axiz Productions](https://www.zedaxiz.com/) provided **hardware + set operators**.

## X-Men: Apocalypse

A nine-projector Pentagon video wall displays missile timers, world maps, world clocks, and aircraft/submarine controls. Dozens of 1980s CRT TVs and computers retrofitted with projectors display custom digital art.

## Quantico / Season 1

A set of **259 Android apps** simulates cellphone calls and texting within 21 episodes. Hundreds of interactive set-prop applications provide video surveillance stations, interactive video walls, an audio surveillance van, command center computers, and bomb timers. Actors operated many of these applications directly for key hero shots.

{{< about-gallery marker="### Quantico, Season 1 by ABC. ###" title="Quantico" description="interactive props + screen applications" >}}

## The Glass Castle

4K/360° video playback supports a large rear-projection setup for shooting car scenes in a film studio. The software includes color calibration, video warping, and wireless playback controls from an Android tablet.

## mother!

Set visualization software lets the director and cinematographer frame shots and block actors. The virtual camera uses real-world measurements and physically based lens settings, with Mac, Windows, and Gear VR versions.
