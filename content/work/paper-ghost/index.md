## Quantico / Season 1

A set of **259 Android apps** simulates cellphone calls and texting within 21 episodes. Hundreds of interactive set-prop applications provide video surveillance stations, interactive video walls, an audio surveillance van, command center computers, and bomb timers. Actors operated many of these applications directly for key hero shots.
