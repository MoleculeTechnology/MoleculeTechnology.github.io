At Paper Ghost, I rapidly developed interactive media for 15 major film + TV productions. While supervising a team of three others, I delivered programming, art, and animation on strict deadlines for key scenes.

I collaborated directly with directors, producers, and VFX supervisors, and worked closely with [Zed Axiz Productions](https://www.zedaxiz.com/). **I provided visuals + software; they provided hardware + set operators.**

## The Glass Castle

I programmed 4K/360° video software for a large rear-projection setup used to shoot car scenes in a film studio. It included color calibration, video warping, and wireless playback controls from an Android tablet.

{{< work-image source="PaperGhost/glasscastle_software1.jpg" alt="A projection test for The Glass Castle, with playback controls and calibration overlays on the driving footage." caption="The Glass Castle: playback + calibration controls." >}}

## X-Men: Apocalypse

I created the Pentagon video wall using nine projectors to show missile timers, world maps, world clocks, and aircraft/submarine controls. I also designed and programmed digital art for dozens of 1980s CRT TVs and computers retrofitted with projectors.

## Quantico / Season 1

I programmed **259 Android apps** to simulate cellphone calls and texting within 21 episodes. I also designed and programmed hundreds of interactive applications for set props, many operated directly by actors for key hero shots.

These included video surveillance stations, interactive video walls, an audio surveillance van, command center computers, and bomb timers.

{{< work-image source="PaperGhost/quantico_van1.jpg" alt="Actors in the Quantico surveillance van surrounded by screen displays and audio equipment." caption="Quantico: screen applications in the audio surveillance van." >}}

## mother!

I programmed set visualization software for the director and cinematographer to frame shots and block actors. The virtual camera used real-world measurements and physically based lens settings, with Mac, Windows, and Gear VR versions.

{{< work-image source="PaperGhost/mother_software_1.jpeg" alt="The mother! previsualization application showing a virtual interior, camera settings, and a connected game controller." caption="mother!: the set previsualization application + virtual camera controls." >}}
