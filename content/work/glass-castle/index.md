4K/360° video playback supports a large rear-projection setup for shooting car scenes in a film studio.

The software includes **color calibration, video warping, and wireless playback controls from an Android tablet**.

[Zed Axiz Productions](https://www.zedaxiz.com/) provided hardware + set operators. Russell's contribution was the playback software and its controls.
