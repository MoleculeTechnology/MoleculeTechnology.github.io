Molecule / Test installation

MOLECULE AT MOONGOAT COFFEE.

A small pop-up installation at Moongoat Coffee tested two approaches to motion capture.

Camera-based body tracking on a vertical TV, with a live wireframe overlay at 60 fps.

Wooden swords fitted with motion sensors and LEDs map real-world orientation to color. Pointing a sword in the same direction produces the same corresponding color.

  • Motion capture
  • Embedded hardware
