# Historical CV review

Source: [Russell Jahn, Resume v15](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1xLfRH0Qsf4-Owy8GTQZyPeFu_Q3kXLMTlJhbCrNWXWY/edit).
Russell identifies this as the CV used when applying to Blizzard and says it is out of date and excludes that experience.
Drive metadata reports a last-modified time in June 2022, which does not make its “NOW” labels current.

Read through the Google Docs connector, including content, table structure, named text styles, and color values.
The browser view required a separate Google sign-in, so a rendered page screenshot was not inspected.
The observations below distinguish documented formatting from judgments about how to adapt it.
The original document has not been changed or copied into this repository.

## What to carry into the website

The CV already has a clear typographic vocabulary.
Its default body face is Roboto Condensed at 9 pt, while large bold caps create a pronounced section hierarchy.
It uses braces, arrows, accent-colored rules, a date column, and structured role/project blocks.
The document background is ghost white (`#F8F8FF`).
The primary accent resolves to approximately `#00B1AE`, with supporting rules near `#3FB6AD`.

Adapt the hierarchy and graphic marks to the requested black website.
Use ghost white for the main display type and mint for selected labels, lines, and links.
Keep the CV's disciplined alignment and strong contrast between heading and detail sizes.
Increase body size and spacing substantially for web reading.
Replace tables used for page positioning with responsive layout.
Use braces and arrows as a recognizable signature in a few places.
Do not import the résumé's dense tool lists and narrow date columns into every project page.

The proposed `#84F9EB` website mint is deliberately lighter than the CV's teal.
Both belong in a palette comparison before the site color is finalized.
The newer request for black, mint, ghost white, and Roboto Condensed takes precedence over incidental historical formatting.

## Content that strengthens the installation story

### InContext deserves a visible supporting story

The CV describes leading engineering and product design for a VR multiplayer installation for a major alcohol distributor.
This is directly relevant installation experience and should not disappear behind the more general ShopperMX editor work.
The current About page also mentions it.
Confirm the final delivered form, client name, dates, interaction, and Russell's responsibilities before publishing a fuller story.

### Embedded work is a continuing thread

The historical Molecule section describes C++ firmware on ESP32 hardware, sensor streaming through WebSockets/UDP, FreeRTOS, local-browser logs, and an iPad application embedding Unity to visualize sensor orientation.
This connects the present firmware work to a longer-running independent practice.
It should inform the narrative, with the old implementation clearly distinguished from the current Saga code and device.
The CV calls the independent role Patchcat. Confirm its relationship to Molecule rather than introducing a second unexplained brand.

### Paper Ghost has unusually concrete spatial examples

The CV describes virtual camera tools using real-world measurements for `mother!`, and Android control of rear projection playback, color calibration, and warping for `The Glass Castle`.
Those details explain how software supported a physical production setup.
They are useful starting points for contribution diagrams and technical captions.

### Enflux supports both interaction and artist tooling

The CV includes the Unity SDK and Blender motion capture/export work, with platforms including mobile, desktop, and XR.
Keep the developer/artist workflow distinct from specific live audience demonstrations.
Historical physical-therapy application descriptions do not establish medical efficacy and need not be the leading artistic portfolio examples.

## Chronology and facts to reconcile

The historical date labels are Patchcat from 2020, InContext from 2019, Enflux 2016–2019, Paper Ghost 2015–2016, and Athgo 2014–2015.
Treat the “NOW” entries as historical wording and confirm actual end dates before creating a public timeline.
Add Blizzard and the present role from the newer career summary and Russell's current account.

The CV describes both overall Quantico work across 22 episodes and 259 smartphone apps used in 21 episodes.
Those statements may refer to different scopes, so verify the distinction before simplifying them to one count.
The CV and public LinkedIn index also differ on the Global Game Jam award year.
Leave the year out of public drafts until confirmed.

The historical CV supplies education details that can be confirmed for the new experience page.
Its phone, email, and other contact details were not copied into these planning notes.

## Recommended résumé direction

Keep a concise employment document with an accurate chronological backbone.
Lead its summary with software engineer and artist, then describe graphics, artist tools, interactive media, and platform depth.
Give Paper Ghost and Enflux concrete project bullets alongside Blizzard.
Use the website for visual case studies and the CV for quick role, responsibility, and achievement scanning.
Carry the same type hierarchy, accent, and graphic vocabulary across both.
