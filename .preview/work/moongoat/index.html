Independent work / Molecule

Light you can move

Light props, motion capture, and an invitation to play. Molecule at a pop-up at Moongoat Coffee.

Contribution
Independent light and motion work, software, and demonstration
Context
A pop-up at Moongoat Coffee

Out of the workshop #

Molecule is where I bring my interests in software, light, and physical interaction together. The pop-up at Moongoat Coffee was a chance to take that work into a shared space.

The setup brought together illuminated sword props, a camera and vertical display, and a small LED face display. The invitation was simple: come play with swords and motion capture.

A connection you can see #

I’m interested in experiences where the relationship between a person’s movement and what they see feels immediate. Light and a physical object offer a different starting point from a conventional screen interface. The object, the visual response, and the space around the participant all become part of the work.

Building the tools to make it #

My independent embedded work includes ESP32 firmware, motion sensor data, networking, and LED experiments. Alongside the physical work, I build the software and tools needed to explore those connections.

This exploration continues through Molecule’s light and sensor projects, with the pop-up forming one chapter in that practice.

Related experience #

At Enflux, I developed motion capture applications and tools that connected people to real-time software and animation workflows. My film-set work brought software and visuals into physical production environments. Those experiences inform the independent work I make as Molecule.
